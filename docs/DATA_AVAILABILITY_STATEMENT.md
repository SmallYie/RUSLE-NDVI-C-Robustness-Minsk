# Data and code availability

## Data availability

Copernicus Sentinel-2 Level-2A surface reflectance and the Shuttle Radar Topography Mission 30 m digital elevation model are available through the Google Earth Engine Data Catalog as [COPERNICUS/S2_SR_HARMONIZED](https://developers.google.com/earth-engine/datasets/catalog/COPERNICUS_S2_SR_HARMONIZED) and [USGS/SRTMGL1_003](https://developers.google.com/earth-engine/datasets/catalog/USGS_SRTMGL1_003). Rainfall erosivity data were obtained from [GloREDa](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.dib.2023.109482). The Belarusian national digital soil map and official land-type layer are subject to the data providers' access conditions. The corresponding author can direct requests to the relevant custodians. Principal derived tabular outputs are included in the manuscript's Online Resource 1.

## Code availability

The Google Earth Engine JavaScript used to compare the prepared 2024 VDK and Linear RUSLE outputs is available under the MIT License in the [SI 2024 repository version](https://github.com/SmallYie/RUSLE-NDVI-C-Robustness-Minsk/tree/si-2024). Reproducing the regional analysis requires access to the two prepared scenario rasters and the corresponding administrative and land-type assets. The [run notes](RUN_NOTES.md) identify the input settings and the thresholds for the revised matched-percentile table.
